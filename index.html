1958
Wichita, Kansas

It started with $600.

Frank and Dan Carney, two college students, borrowed $600 from their mother and opened a small pizza restaurant in their hometown of Wichita, Kansas. Their approach centered on fresh meat and vegetable toppings, with pizzas baked fresh to order.

FROM WICHITA TO THE PHILIPPINES.

1984
Pizza Hut Philippines

The story arrives in the Philippines.

Pizza Hut began operating in the Philippines in 1984. The official history describes its presence expanding through Metro Manila and surrounding provinces, and into the Visayas and Mindanao through dine-in restaurants, delivery units, and express counters.

Pizza Hut Philippines beginsVisual dramatization
1989
Delivery begins

Pizza started coming to the customer.

Pizza Hut began its delivery service in the Philippines in 1989 through Restaurant Based Delivery units at Quad Parksquare in Makati and at Delta, Quezon Avenue corner West Avenue in Quezon City.

Delivery eraVisual dramatization
1990
DELCO

A store designed for delivery and carryout.

Growing demand led to the opening of the first Delivery and Carryout, or DELCO, unit in Greenhills, San Juan in 1990. The format was designed mainly for delivery and carryout customers, while still providing facilities for people who wanted to eat in the store.

The idea behind the success.

The official Pizza Hut Philippines history highlights a consistent focus on product quality, service, cleanliness, and value.

Quality

Fresh dough, signature tomato sauce, meat and vegetable toppings, and mozzarella are emphasized in the brand's account of its product approach.

Service

Crew members are trained to make customers feel appreciated through courtesy, attentiveness, respect, and enthusiasm.

Cleanliness

Cleanliness is presented as a core operating expectation alongside service and product quality.

Value

The history frames customer satisfaction as the result of delivering strong product, service, cleanliness, and value together.
